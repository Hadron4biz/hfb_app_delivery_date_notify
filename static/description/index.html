Delivery Date Change Notification

A delivery date changes - your salespeople get a one-click email ready to send.

The problem

A delivery slips, or gets pulled forward, on an order that's already been confirmed - and someone has to remember to tell the customer. In practice that means hunting down their email address and writing a message by hand, or - more often - forgetting altogether.

How it works

1

Delivery date changes

On a confirmed order, the order is flagged automatically.

2

Two buttons appear

Send a notification, pre-filled with the right template - or dismiss it.

3

One click, done

Review the email, hit send. The flag clears itself automatically.

What you get

Two ready-made templates

One for a delayed delivery, one for an earlier one - picked automatically, and fully editable like any other Odoo email template.

Nothing sends itself

The salesperson always reviews the message in the composer before it goes out - or dismisses it if the customer already knows.

No repeat notifications

If the date bounces back to what the customer already knows, the flag clears on its own - nothing to send.

Easy to find

A dedicated search filter surfaces every order with a delivery date change still waiting on a decision.

Where it applies

Confirmed sale orders (sale.order) with a customer set. Requires the Sales app. No new models, no extra configuration, no security rules - install it and the buttons show up when a delivery date actually changes.
